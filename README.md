# 💫 About Me

I bridge the gap between complex operational bottlenecks and high-performance engineering. I specialize in building robust web applications designed from the ground up for maximum scale, strict efficiency, and concrete business value.

* 🛒 **Hybrid E-commerce:** Engineering lightning-fast headless architectures combining custom Next.js frontends with robust Shopify backends.
* 🏢 **Enterprise Architecture (ERP & CRM):** Designing custom Operational ERPs and CRMs from scratch, with deep expertise in Vendor Management Systems (VMS), customer pipelines, and automated Booking/Dispatch workflows.
